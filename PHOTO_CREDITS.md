The JiTap logo and mascot are pre-existing JiTap assets used with permission through this project. Mascot is displayed at or below its current WebP intrinsic resolution (165 px wide) to avoid upscaling blur.

## Asset engineering
- The site no longer displays 50px-tall photo strips enlarged into 100px cards.
- Individual high-resolution 450–1050px service photos are loaded responsively from Unsplash's image CDN, with native object-fit cropping and a Mombasa coastal hero loaded at 2400px.
- Browser QA asserts images load at 350px+ native width and never upscale beyond their actual resolution.
